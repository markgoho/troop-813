# Troop 813

Websites and web apps supporting Scouts BSA Troop 813 of Spencerport, NY.

## People

**Scout**:
A youth member of Troop 813 on the troop roster.
_Avoid_: Member, kid, user

**Leader**:
An adult who runs troop business and can use troop-only screens.
_Avoid_: Scoutmaster (a specific Leader role, not the general term)

**Admin**:
The one Leader who grants Leader access, starts Meetings and unlocks the Meeting Kiosk.
_Avoid_: Super admin, owner

**Parent**:
An adult who signs in but isn't a Leader.
_Avoid_: Guardian, user

**Roster**:
The list of current Scouts, by first and last name.
_Avoid_: Member list, directory

## Gatherings

**Meeting**:
The weekly troop gathering, Monday nights 7:00–8:30pm at the Troop 92 lodge in Spencerport. Scouts record Attendance at Meetings; they do not RSVP to them.
_Avoid_: Event (a different concept)

**Event**:
A non-Meeting troop activity a Scout RSVPs to, typically a monthly Fri–Sun Campout.
_Avoid_: Meeting, activity, outing

**Campout**:
The most common kind of Event: an overnight trip, typically Friday through Sunday.

**Attendance**:
The record that a Scout was present at a particular Meeting.
_Avoid_: Check-in (the act; Attendance is the record)

**RSVP**:
A Scout's answer to whether they will go to a particular Event.
_Avoid_: Registration, sign-up

## Meeting Kiosk

**Meeting Kiosk**:
The shared-tablet screen a Leader unlocks at a Meeting so Scouts can record Attendance and RSVP in turn.
_Avoid_: Attendance page, check-in app

**Handoff**:
The step where the Meeting Kiosk names a specific Scout for the current Scout to find and pass the tablet to.
_Avoid_: Pass-off, relay
