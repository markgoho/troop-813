Meeting Kiosk flow

Question: how should the Kiosk behave end to end at a Meeting? First Scout, the "Are you …?" Handoff, RSVP prompts, "I can't find them", late arrivals, the end of the chain, and a Leader fixing a wrong check-in. Tap the tablet directly, or run a walkthrough on the right. Open with #tablet on the end of the link to see only the Kiosk screen (for the Pixel Tablet).

Defaults built in, to agree or disagree with:

  • Attendance is recorded the moment a Scout is confirmed, before any RSVP questions. Walking away mid-RSVP still counts.
  • The Handoff screen and "Are you Caleb?" are one screen: Yes / No, I'm someone else / I can't find Caleb.
  • Handoff order is a ring: the next Scout after the current one (alphabetical by first name, or a random order fixed when the Kiosk is unlocked), skipping anyone checked in or not found.
  • A Scout marked "can't find" is not named again that Meeting. They can still tap their name on the list.
  • RSVP answers: Going / Not going / Ask me next week. "Ask me next week" records nothing, so it asks again at the next Meeting. There is no "Maybe".
  • A Scout is asked about every upcoming Event with no answer. Then a summary lets them change any answer.
  • When the tablet sits idle, or the ring runs out, the Kiosk falls back to one resting screen: the name list.
  • A Leader's "Undo check-in" also rolls back the RSVP answers given during that check-in.
