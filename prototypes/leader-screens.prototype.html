Leader screens on a phone

Three navigation variants of the same eight screens. Flip with the black bar at the bottom or the ← → keys. Switch role and Kiosk state above the phone. Everything is in memory, so reload to reset. It's Monday Nov 9, 7:25pm, and tonight's Meeting is live on the tablet.

Variant

Defaults to react to

  1. Live Meeting: one list of checked-in Scouts (Undo) and not checked in (Check in). Undo opens an inline confirm that names the RSVP answers it will roll back.
  2. Kiosk asleep shows as a warning banner with one Wake Kiosk button, at the top of the live view.
  3. No Meeting today: the phone says the Admin starts it on the Kiosk. The phone can only create Meetings for past dates.
  4. Past Meeting: pick a date (defaults to the latest Monday with no Meeting), then tap Scouts to toggle present. Toggling off is an undo. Delete appears once nobody is present.
  5. RSVP list: every active Scout with Going / Not going / No answer. "No answer" clears it, so the Kiosk asks again. Tap a name for the answer history.
  6. Scout page: "Present X of Y" since the Scout was added, then each Meeting newest first, then their RSVPs.
  7. Deleting a Scout or Event: the button is always shown, disabled with the reason when it's in use. Deactivate / Cancel are the normal path.
  8. Admin: a Users list with a Leader switch per person. New sign-ins sit at the top as "Waiting".
  9. Parent: one waiting page, no navigation.
